How Coach Analyzes Your Work

BrushTutor is designed differently from normal drawing tools. Instead of using AI to draw for you, BrushTutor mimics a professional teacher looking over your shoulder.

1. Structure Detection: The coach checks the skeletal form and construction lines to analyze proportions and anatomy structure.

2. Light Source & Depth: It attempts to recognize where your highlights and shadow blocks fall, identifying if they line up logically.

3. Perspective Review: The coach reviews your lines relative to perspective grids, highlighting horizon and vanishing point misalignments.
